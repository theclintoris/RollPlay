## CA certificate bundle

RollPlay ships `assets/certs/cacert.pem` so HTTPS requests to the public Steam
Store API verify consistently on every machine. The bundle is the Mozilla CA
certificate store as curated by the curl project.

- Source: https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem
- Mozilla CA certificate policy: https://www.mozilla.org/projects/security/certs/policy/
- License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 — full text in `licenses/MPL-2.0.txt`.

The certificate bundle is data used for TLS verification. It is not modified by
RollPlay.
